About

Challenge

Increase conversion rate to applicants without loss of quality of filling profiles.
Refresh of UI.
Make it the minimum allowable resources.

Search

Research

24% of users left the site after getting search results.
Some of the requests of the users showed no results. Input area for location looks like required and users can't see results from neighbor cantons, however, some are ready for visiting work there.
The site is not a job aggregator and doesn't have >10k vacancies, early filtering limits the search results.

Solutions

Was decided to change the realization of the search input area. Location becomes like additional function.

Involvement blocks

Research

The most popular page for entering is home page, but 21% of users left the site there.

Solutions

Suggest to users more helpful information

  • The latest added vacancies are filtered by area and location.
  • Statistics on the number of vacancies by city and industry,

Applying form

Research

55% of visitors didn't fill it out and left this page.
To find a solution was analyzed application processing process of recruiters. Were found one more issue: Some recruiters have to ask for references and certificates from applicants additionally because they didn't attach them in apply.

Solutions

Hide some input areas with not important information on the first step.
Add split types of documents in the attached form.
