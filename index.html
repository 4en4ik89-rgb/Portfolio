Search

Research

24% of users left the site after getting search results.
Some of the requests of the users showed no results. Input area for location looks like required and users can't see results from neighbor cantons, however, some are ready for visiting work there.
The site is not a job aggregator and doesn't have >10k vacancies, early filtering limits the search results.

Solutions

Was decided to change the realization of the search input area. Location becomes like additional function.

Problem statement

A doctor’s prescription includes more than one medicine

According to the survey, people were using two options.

Offline 76%

Users are required to visit multiple pharmacies to purchase all of the items on their list, which makes it difficult for them to compare prices and find the best deals.

Online 24%

Booking medicine one by one can be time-consuming, and it may also require picking up items from multiple pharmacies

Solution

Make a new feature “List of goods”
